The Suit Plan

An evening suit — not a full black-tie tuxedo. Inky dark green wool, notch lapel, turndown collar, straight tie. The two groomsmen in charcoal with notch lapels and dark green ties, so they read as a set without pretending to match me.

One

My suit — dark green, notch lapel, matte wool

The colour I'm after is inky and almost-black in low light — hunter/forest green rather than anything bright or olive. Matte wool, self-faced notch lapel. It reads as formal because of the cut and the darkness, not because of satin.

Two

The three details that keep it a suit, not a tux

These are the calls that matter. Each one dials the formality down just enough that the groomsmen in charcoal suits stand next to me without looking underdressed.

Collar

YesTurndown — a normal point/spread dress-shirt collar.
NoWing collar. It's black-tie-or-nothing and looks costumey in photos.

Neckwear

YesStraight tie in dark green silk.
NoBow tie. It instantly re-classifies the whole outfit as black tie.

Lapel

YesNotch lapel, matching what the groomsmen wear.
NoPeak or shawl. Both are formalwear signals that break the set.
Three

The groomsmen — charcoal, notch lapel

Charcoal is the right neighbour to dark green: it's dark enough to hold its own in evening light, it isn't trying to be a second colour statement, and a charcoal suit next to a green one reads as a deliberate pairing rather than a mismatch. Same lapel shape as mine, so the shoulders line up in every photo.

Four

Dark green ties for the groomsmen

The green tie is the thread that ties them to me. Same family as my suit, in silk. Grenadine (an open, textured silk weave) is my preference — it's matte, has visible depth, and photographs as texture instead of a flat block of colour.

Five

Why this works together

The trick is that everyone sits on the same rung of the formality ladder, and only the colour tells you who's who.

One step apart, not one world apart

If I wore a proper tuxedo — peak or shawl lapel, wing collar, bow tie — the groomsmen in suits would look like guests. Every choice below closes that gap.
